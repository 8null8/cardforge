    Getting your picks from 5e.tools

    Copy links
    Open any spell, creature, item or feat on 5e.tools and copy the address. Paste as many as you like.
    Pinned lists
    On a 5e.tools list page, pin entries, then use Download Pinned List under the pinned list and open that file here.
    The list collector
    A small Tampermonkey script that adds an Add button to every 5e.tools page, plus class features, whole filtered lists and loot-generator results, and sends your list straight here. Install the collector It sends lists to this site. If yours sends them elsewhere, click change under its list and use

    Card Forge reads the same public data files 5e.tools uses and keeps a copy in your browser for a week. Your list stays in this browser. Card text belongs to its publishers; print it for your own table.
